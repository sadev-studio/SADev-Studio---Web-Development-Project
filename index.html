Behind The Curtain

Ten Facts

01

The show was created by twin brothers Matt and Ross Duffer, known collectively as "The Duffer Brothers."

02

Millie Bobby Brown was cast as Eleven when she was just eleven years old.

03

The Upside Down's decaying, fungal visual style drew inspiration from mold and spore growth.

04

Kate Bush's decades-old song "Running Up That Hill" saw a massive streaming resurgence after Season 4.

05

Steve Harrington was originally conceived as a minor antagonist before becoming a fan-favorite protector.

06

The Snow Ball dance scene leaned heavily on genuine, mostly unscripted chemistry between the young cast.

07

The show's glowing red title typography became one of the most recognized pieces of pop-culture branding of its decade.

08

A stage play prequel, "Stranger Things: The First Shadow," expanded the story beyond the screen.

09

Season 5 closed the story nearly a decade after the first season premiered.

10

An animated spin-off, set in 1985, was announced to continue the world after the live-action finale.
