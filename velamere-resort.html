Our Story

A sanctuary built
into the rock.

Velamere was carved, not constructed — into a limestone peninsula where three generations of fishermen once kept their boats. Every terrace faces west. Every room hears the water.

We keep the property small on purpose. No wing feels far from the sea, no evening feels far from a long dinner table.
