Questions

Frequently asked.

What is the minimum account size?+

Our private client tier begins at $250,000. Institutional and fund accounts are structured individually — speak with an advisor for details.

How is custody secured?+

Assets are held in multi-signature cold storage across geographically distributed vaults, insured, and independently audited each quarter with public proof-of-reserves.

What fees does Obsidian charge?+

Management fees are tiered by AUM and disclosed in full before onboarding. There are no hidden spreads on OTC execution — pricing is quoted transparently, trade by trade.

Which jurisdictions do you operate in?+

Obsidian is licensed and regulated across multiple jurisdictions and currently serves clients in 27 countries. Availability varies by local regulation.

Can I withdraw at any time?+

Yes. There are no lock-up periods on custody or managed portfolio assets outside of active yield strategies, which disclose terms upfront.
